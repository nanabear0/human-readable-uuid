Under the hood

Eight chunks.
Every bit preserved.

Each 16-bit UUID chunk becomes one word from a fixed 2,048-word dictionary plus a number from 00 to 31. The result is longer, but easier for humans to distinguish and communicate.

128-bit UUID ÷ 8 chunks → word11 bits + number5 bits

Slopcoded end to end. Use thoughtfully. Read the limitations.
